---
name: grilling
description: Grill the user relentlessly about a plan, decision, or idea. Use when the user wants to stress-test their thinking, or uses any 'grill' trigger phrases.
---

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down each branch of the decision tree, ordering the questions by their dependencies.

If a *fact* can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up first rather than asking me. The *decisions*, though, are mine.

Then ask ALL the questions in ONE numbered message: one line per question, with your recommended answer on that line. I answer in bulk. Only the questions born from my answers make a second round.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
